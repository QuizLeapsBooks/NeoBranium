Fair Ranking Algorithm

NeoBranium ranks learners on genuine mastery and consistency, not simply the sheer quantity of quiz attempts.

Correct Answers

+10 points per verified correct answer in authentic chapter quizzes.

Accuracy Bonus

Up to +500 points multiplier for high accuracy across all attempts.

Chapter Mastery

+50 points per distinct chapter where you achieve 80%+ score.

Study Consistency

+15 points per active streak day (capped at 30 days) and active visit days.

Spam & Repeat Protection

Repeatedly retaking identical or easy quizzes provides diminishing returns. Ranks require a minimum of 3 distinct quizzes and 15 questions before competitive rank assignment.

Privacy Notice

Profiles on this leaderboard are 100% voluntary. NeoBranium never displays emails, phone numbers, passwords, private notes, or chat logs. You can switch your profile to private at any time in Settings.
