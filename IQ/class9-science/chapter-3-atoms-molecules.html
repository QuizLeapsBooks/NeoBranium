Q1 1 Mark CBSE Annual Exam MCQ

The chemical formula of Aluminium Sulphate is:

  • (A) AlSO4
  • (B) Al2(SO4)3
  • (C) Al3(SO4)2
  • (D) Al2SO4
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:

Correct Option: (B)

Aluminium has valency +3 (Al3+), sulphate has valency -2 (SO42-). Criss-crossing valencies gives Al2(SO4)3.

Exam Tip: Criss-cross method: Al (3) and SO4 (2) -> Al2(SO4)3.
Q2 2 Marks CBSE Annual Exam Laws of Chemical Combination

State the Law of Conservation of Mass and the Law of Constant Proportions.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
1. Law of Conservation of Mass (Lavoisier, 1774):

Mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. Total mass of reactants equals total mass of products.

2. Law of Constant Proportions (Proust, 1779):

In a chemical substance, the constituent elements are always present in a definite proportion by mass. E.g., in water (H2O), hydrogen and oxygen are always in 1 : 8 ratio by mass.

Exam Tip: In water, ratio of H to O is always 1:8 by mass (2g H to 16g O).
Q3 3 Marks CBSE Annual Exam Chemical Formula Writing

Write the chemical formulae for the following compounds using the criss-cross method:

  1. Magnesium Chloride
  2. Calcium Carbonate
  3. Sodium Oxide
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
  1. Magnesium Chloride: Mg2+ and Cl- → MgCl2
  2. Calcium Carbonate: Ca2+ and CO32- → Ca2(CO3)2 → CaCO3
  3. Sodium Oxide: Na+ and O2- → Na2O
Exam Tip: When charges are equal (Ca2+ and CO3 2-), simplify ratio 2:2 to 1:1.
Q4 3 Marks CBSE Annual Exam Molecular Mass

Calculate the molecular mass of:

  1. Nitric Acid (HNO3)
  2. Glucose (C6H12O6)

(Atomic masses: H = 1 u, N = 14 u, O = 16 u, C = 12 u)

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
1. Nitric Acid (HNO3):
Mass = 1(H) + 1(N) + 3(O) = 1(1) + 1(14) + 3(16) = 1 + 14 + 48 = 63 u
2. Glucose (C6H12O6):
Mass = 6(12) + 12(1) + 6(16) = 72 + 12 + 96 = 180 u
Exam Tip: Always write atomic mass unit 'u' (unified mass) in final answer.
