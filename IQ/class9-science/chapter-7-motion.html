Q1 1 Mark CBSE Annual Exam MCQ

A body moves around a circular path of radius r. What is its displacement after completing one and a half (1.5) rounds?

  • (A) 2πr
  • (B) πr
  • (C) 2r
  • (D) 0
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:

Correct Option: (C)

After completing 1.5 rounds, the body reaches the diametrically opposite point from its starting point. The shortest distance between initial and final position is the diameter: 2r.

Exam Tip: Displacement is the straight-line distance between initial and final points, not distance travelled.
Q2 2 Marks CBSE Annual Exam Short Answer

Differentiate between Distance and Displacement.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
  • Distance: Actual total path length travelled by an object. It is a scalar quantity and is always positive (> 0).
  • Displacement: Shortest straight-line vector from the initial position to the final position. It can be positive, negative, or zero.
Exam Tip: Distance >= |Displacement|. Displacement can be zero even if distance is non-zero.
Q3 3 Marks CBSE Annual Exam Numerical

A bus starting from rest accelerates uniformly at 0.1 m/s2 for 2 minutes. Find:

  1. The speed acquired.
  2. The distance travelled.
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Given:

u = 0, a = 0.1 m/s2, t = 2 min = 2 × 60 = 120 s.

  1. Speed acquired (v):
    v = u + at = 0 + (0.1 × 120) = 12 m/s
  2. Distance travelled (s):
    s = ut + ½ at2 = 0 + ½ (0.1) × (120)2 = 0.05 × 14400 = 720 m

Speed acquired is 12 m/s and distance travelled is 720 m.

Exam Tip: Always convert time into seconds before using equations of motion.
Q4 5 Marks CBSE Annual Exam Derivation

Derive the second equation of motion, s = ut + ½ at2, graphically from a velocity-time graph for a uniformly accelerated body.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Graphical Derivation:

Consider a body moving with initial velocity u at t = 0 (point A), accelerating uniformly to final velocity v at time t (point B). Draw velocity-time graph with line AB.

The distance travelled (s) is equal to the area under the velocity-time graph (Area of trapezium OABD):

s = Area of rectangle OACD + Area of triangle ABD
Area of rectangle OACD = OA × OD = u × t = ut
Area of ΔABD = ½ × Base × Height = ½ × AD × BD
AD = t, and BD = v - u = at (from acceleration formula a = (v-u)/t)
Area of ΔABD = ½ × t × (at) = ½ at2

Total distance:

s = ut + ½ at2

Hence derived.

Exam Tip: Area under v-t graph = Distance travelled.
