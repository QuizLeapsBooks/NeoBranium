Q1 1 Mark CBSE 2024, 2022 MCQ

If one zero of the quadratic polynomial x2 + 3x + k is 2, then the value of k is:

  • (A) 10
  • (B) -10
  • (C) -7
  • (D) -2
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:

Correct Option: (B)

p(2) = (2)2 + 3(2) + k = 0 → 4 + 6 + k = 0 → k = -10
Board Exam Tip: Substitute zero directly into polynomial.
Q2 1 Mark CBSE 2023, 2021 MCQ

The number of zeroes of a polynomial y = p(x) whose graph intersects the x-axis at 3 distinct points is:

  • (A) 1
  • (B) 2
  • (C) 3
  • (D) 4
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:

Correct Option: (C)

The number of real zeroes of y = p(x) is equal to the number of points where the graph intersects or touches the x-axis. Since it intersects at 3 points, it has 3 zeroes.

Board Exam Tip: Count only intersections with the x-axis, ignore the y-axis.
Q3 2 Marks CBSE 2024, 2020 Short Answer

Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are (2 + √3) and (2 - √3).

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Calculate Sum and Product of Zeroes:
Sum (S) = (2 + √3) + (2 - √3) = 4
Product (P) = (2 + √3)(2 - √3) = (2)2 - (√3)2 = 4 - 3 = 1
Quadratic Polynomial Formula:
p(x) = k[x2 - Sx + P] = x2 - 4x + 1

The required polynomial is x2 - 4x + 1.

Board Exam Tip: Use (a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2 for product.
Q4 2 Marks CBSE 2023, 2019 Verification

Find the zeroes of p(x) = 4s2 - 4s + 1 and verify the relationship between zeroes and coefficients.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Zeroes:
4s2 - 4s + 1 = (2s - 1)2 = 0 → s = 1/2, 1/2

α = 1/2, β = 1/2. Here a = 4, b = -4, c = 1.

  • Sum: α + β = 1/2 + 1/2 = 1 = -b/a = -(-4)/4 = 1 (Verified)
  • Product: αβ = 1/4 = c/a = 1/4 (Verified)
Board Exam Tip: Always write both sum and product verification explicitly.
Q5 3 Marks CBSE 2024, 2022 Algebraic Evaluation

If α and β are zeroes of x2 - 5x + 6, find α2 + β2 and 1/α + 1/β.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Calculations:

α + β = 5, αβ = 6.

α2 + β2 = (α + β)2 - 2αβ = (5)2 - 2(6) = 25 - 12 = 13
1/α + 1/β = (α + β) / αβ = 5 / 6
Board Exam Tip: Express expressions in terms of (alpha+beta) and (alpha*beta).
Q6 3 Marks CBSE 2023, 2018 Forming New Polynomial

If α and β are the zeroes of p(x) = 2x2 - 4x + 5, form a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 2α and 2β.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Step 1: Original Zeroes:
α + β = -(-4) / 2 = 2
αβ = 5 / 2
Step 2: New Zeroes S' and P':
S' = 2α + 2β = 2(α + β) = 2(2) = 4
P' = (2α)(2β) = 4αβ = 4(5/2) = 10
New Polynomial:
p'(x) = x2 - S'x + P' = x2 - 4x + 10
Board Exam Tip: Calculate new sum S' and new product P' first.
Q7 4 Marks CBSE 2024, 2019 Unknown Constant (k)

If the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial f(x) = kx2 + 4x + 4 are α and β such that α2 + β2 = 24, find the value of k.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Solution:

α + β = -4/k, αβ = 4/k.

(α + β)2 - 2αβ = 24
16/k2 - 8/k = 24 → 3k2 + k - 2 = 0
(3k - 2)(k + 1) = 0 → k = 2/3 or k = -1

k = 2/3 or k = -1.

Board Exam Tip: Factorize the quadratic in k.
