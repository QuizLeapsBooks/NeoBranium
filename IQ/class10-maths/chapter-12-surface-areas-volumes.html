Q1 1 Mark CBSE 2024, 2022 MCQ

Two cubes each of volume 64 cm3 are joined end to end. The surface area of the resulting cuboid is:

  • (A) 128 cm2
  • (B) 160 cm2
  • (C) 192 cm2
  • (D) 256 cm2
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:

Correct Option: (B)

Edge = 4 cm. Resulting cuboid: l = 8 cm, b = 4 cm, h = 4 cm.
TSA = 2(32 + 16 + 32) = 160 cm2.

Board Exam Tip: TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl).
Q2 3 Marks CBSE 2024, 2021 Toy TSA Problem

A toy is in the form of a cone of radius 3.5 cm mounted on a hemisphere of same radius. The total height of the toy is 15.5 cm. Find the total surface area of the toy. (Take π = 22/7)

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Dimensions:

r = 3.5 cm = 7/2 cm, h = 15.5 - 3.5 = 12 cm.
Slant height l = √(122 + 3.52) = 12.5 cm.

TSA = πrl + 2πr2 = πr(l + 2r) = (22/7) × (7/2) × (12.5 + 7) = 11 × 19.5 = 214.5 cm2
Board Exam Tip: TSA of toy = CSA of cone + CSA of hemisphere.
Q3 4 Marks CBSE 2023, 2019 Conical Tent Problem

A tent is in the shape of a cylinder surmounted by a conical top. If the height and diameter of the cylindrical part are 2.1 m and 4 m respectively, and the slant height of the top is 2.8 m, find the area of the canvas used for making the tent. Also, find the cost of the canvas at the rate of ₹500 per m2. (Note: Base of tent is not covered with canvas).

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Dimensions:
  • Radius r = 4/2 = 2 m
  • Height of cylinder h = 2.1 m
  • Slant height of cone l = 2.8 m
Area of Canvas:
Area = CSA of cylinder + CSA of cone
= 2πrh + πrl = πr(2h + l)
= (22/7) × 2 × [2(2.1) + 2.8]
= (44/7) × [4.2 + 2.8] = (44/7) × 7 = 44 m2
Cost of Canvas:
Cost = 44 m2 × ₹500 = ₹22,000
Board Exam Tip: Notice (4.2 + 2.8) = 7.0 which cancels nicely with 7 in 22/7.
Q4 5 Marks CBSE 2024, 2018 Capsule Problem

A medicine capsule is in the shape of a cylinder with two hemispheres stuck to each of its ends. Total length is 14 mm and diameter is 5 mm. Find its surface area and capacity (volume).

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Calculations:

r = 2.5 mm, h = 14 - 5 = 9 mm.

Surface Area = 2πrh + 4πr2 = 2πr(h + 2r) = 2 × (22/7) × 2.5 × 14 = 220 mm2
Volume = πr2h + (4/3)πr3 ≈ 242.2 mm3
Board Exam Tip: Two hemispheres equal one complete sphere.
