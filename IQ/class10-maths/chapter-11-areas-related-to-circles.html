Q1 1 Mark CBSE 2024, 2022 MCQ

If the perimeter and area of a circle are numerically equal, then the radius of the circle is:

  • (A) 2 units
  • (B) π units
  • (C) 4 units
  • (D) 7 units
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:

Correct Option: (A)

2πr = πr2 → r = 2 units.

Board Exam Tip: 2*pi*r = pi*r^2 gives r = 2.
Q2 2 Marks CBSE 2023, 2021 Short Answer

The length of the minute hand of a clock is 14 cm. Find the area swept by the minute hand in 5 minutes. (Take π = 22/7)

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Calculation:

Angle in 5 minutes = (5 / 60) × 360° = 30°.

Area = (30/360) × (22/7) × 14 × 14 = (1/12) × 616 = 154 / 3 cm2 ≈ 51.33 cm2
Board Exam Tip: Angle swept in 1 minute is 6 degrees.
Q3 3 Marks CBSE 2024, 2020 Horse Grazing Problem

A horse is tied to a peg at one corner of a square grass field of side 15 m by means of a 5 m long rope. Find:

  1. The area of that part of the field in which the horse can graze.
  2. The increase in grazing area if the rope were 10 m long instead of 5 m. (Use π = 3.14)
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
1. Grazing Area with 5 m rope:

Corner of square forms a quadrant (θ = 90°):

Area1 = (90/360) × π × (5)2 = (1/4) × 3.14 × 25 = 19.625 m2
2. Grazing Area with 10 m rope:
Area2 = (1/4) × 3.14 × (10)2 = (1/4) × 314 = 78.5 m2
Increase in area = 78.5 - 19.625 = 58.875 m2

The increase in grazing area is 58.875 m2.

Board Exam Tip: Corner of a square field subtends 90 degrees, forming a quarter circle (quadrant).
Q4 4 Marks CBSE 2023, 2019 Segment Area

A chord of a circle of radius 15 cm subtends an angle of 60° at the centre. Find the areas of the corresponding minor and major segments of the circle. (Use π = 3.14 and √3 = 1.73)

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Calculations:
Area of sector = (60/360) × 3.14 × 15 × 15 = 117.75 cm2
Area of ΔOAB = (√3 / 4) × 152 = (1.73 × 225)/4 = 97.3125 cm2
Area of minor segment = 117.75 - 97.3125 = 20.4375 cm2
Area of circle = 3.14 × 225 = 706.5 cm2
Area of major segment = 706.5 - 20.4375 = 686.0625 cm2
Board Exam Tip: Minor segment = Sector area - Triangle area.
