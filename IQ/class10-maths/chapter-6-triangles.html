Q1 1 Mark CBSE 2024, 2022 MCQ

In ΔABC, DE || BC such that AD = 3 cm, DB = 5 cm, and AE = 1.8 cm. The length of EC is:

  • (A) 2.5 cm
  • (B) 3.0 cm
  • (C) 3.5 cm
  • (D) 4.0 cm
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:

Correct Option: (B)

By Thales Theorem (BPT): AD / DB = AE / EC

3 / 5 = 1.8 / EC → 3 × EC = 9.0 → EC = 3.0 cm
Board Exam Tip: BPT formula: AD/DB = AE/EC.
Q2 1 Mark CBSE 2023, 2020 MCQ

If ΔABC ~ ΔEDF and ΔABC is not similar to ΔDEF, then which of the following is NOT true?

  • (A) BC × EF = AC × FD
  • (B) AB × EF = AC × DE
  • (C) BC × DE = AB × EF
  • (D) BC × DF = AB × EF
Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:

Correct Option: (C)

Since ΔABC ~ ΔEDF, corresponding sides are proportional in order:

AB / ED = BC / DF = AC / EF

From AB / ED = BC / DF → BC × ED = AB × DF (NOT AB × EF). Hence (C) is false.

Board Exam Tip: Match letter order strictly: ΔABC ~ ΔEDF means A<->E, B<->D, C<->F.
Q3 2 Marks CBSE 2024, 2021 Short Answer

In ΔABC, D and E are points on sides AB and AC such that AD = 2 cm, BD = 3 cm, AE = 3.5 cm, and AC = 8.75 cm. State whether DE || BC or not with justification.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Calculate Ratios:

EC = AC - AE = 8.75 - 3.5 = 5.25 cm

AD / DB = 2 / 3 ≈ 0.667
AE / EC = 3.5 / 5.25 = 350 / 525 = (7 × 50) / (7 × 75) = 2 / 3

Since AD / DB = AE / EC = 2/3, by the Converse of Basic Proportionality Theorem, DE is parallel to BC (DE || BC).

Board Exam Tip: Converse of BPT: If a line divides two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, the line is parallel to the third side.
Q4 2 Marks CBSE 2023, 2019 Shadow Similarity

A vertical pole of length 6 m casts a shadow 4 m long on the ground and at the same time a tower casts a shadow 28 m long. Find the height of the tower.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Solution:

Both vertical objects form right-angled triangles with the same sun angle θ (AA similarity):

Height of pole / Height of tower = Shadow of pole / Shadow of tower
6 / h = 4 / 28 = 1 / 7 → h = 6 × 7 = 42 m

The height of the tower is 42 metres.

Board Exam Tip: Sun's angular elevation is the same at any given moment.
Q5 3 Marks CBSE 2024, 2020 Trapezium Proof

Diagonals of a trapezium ABCD with AB || DC intersect each other at point O. Using similarity of triangles, show that AO / BO = CO / DO.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Proof:

In ΔAOB and ΔCOD:

  • ∠OAB = ∠OCD (Alternate interior angles, AB || DC)
  • ∠OBA = ∠ODC (Alternate interior angles, AB || DC)
  • ∠AOB = ∠COD (Vertically opposite angles)

By AAA Similarity: ΔAOB ~ ΔCOD

AO / CO = BO / DO → AO / BO = CO / DO

Hence proved.

Board Exam Tip: Alternate interior angles formed by parallel sides prove similarity instantly.
Q6 3 Marks CBSE 2022, 2018 Angle Calculation

In the given figure, ΔODC ~ ΔOBA, ∠BOC = 125° and ∠CDO = 70°. Find ∠DOC, ∠DCO, and ∠OAB.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Calculations:
  1. DOB is a straight line → ∠DOC + ∠BOC = 180° (Linear pair)
    ∠DOC = 180° - 125° = 55°
  2. In ΔODC: Sum of angles = 180°
    ∠DCO = 180° - (∠DOC + ∠CDO) = 180° - (55° + 70°) = 180° - 125° = 55°
    ∠DCO = 55°
  3. Since ΔODC ~ ΔOBA, corresponding angles are equal:
    ∠OAB = ∠OCD = ∠DCO = 55°

∠DOC = 55°, ∠DCO = 55°, ∠OAB = 55°.

Board Exam Tip: Linear pair gives ∠DOC. Then angle sum of triangle gives ∠DCO.
Q7 5 Marks CBSE 2024, 2023, 2019 Theorem Proof (Thales Theorem)

State and Prove the Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales Theorem).

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Statement:

If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, the other two sides are divided in the same ratio.

Proof:

Given ΔABC with DE || BC intersecting AB at D and AC at E. Join BE, CD. Draw DM ⊥ AC and EN ⊥ AB.

Area(ΔADE) / Area(ΔBDE) = (½ × AD × EN) / (½ × DB × EN) = AD / DB … (1)
Area(ΔADE) / Area(ΔDEC) = (½ × AE × DM) / (½ × EC × DM) = AE / EC … (2)

Since ΔBDE and ΔDEC lie on the same base DE and between the same parallel lines DE and BC:
Area(ΔBDE) = Area(ΔDEC) … (3)

From (1), (2), and (3):

AD / DB = AE / EC

Hence proved.

Board Exam Tip: Must draw neat diagram with constructions (perpendiculars DM and EN, dotted joins BE and CD).
Q8 5 Marks CBSE 2024 Sample Paper Case Study / Street Lamp

A girl of height 90 cm is walking away from the base of a lamp-post at a speed of 1.2 m/s. If the lamp is 3.6 m above the ground, find the length of her shadow after 4 seconds.

Step-by-Step Solution & Marking Scheme:
Step 1: Convert units and find distance walked:
  • Height of lamp-post AB = 3.6 m = 360 cm
  • Height of girl CD = 90 cm
  • Distance walked BD = Speed × Time = 1.2 m/s × 4 s = 4.8 m = 480 cm
  • Let length of her shadow DE = x cm
Step 2: Similar Triangles:

In ΔABE and ΔCDE:
∠B = ∠D = 90°
∠E = ∠E (Common angle)
Therefore, ΔABE ~ ΔCDE (AA similarity)

AB / CD = BE / DE
360 / 90 = (BD + DE) / DE
4 = (4.8 + x) / x
4x = 4.8 + x → 3x = 4.8 → x = 1.6 m

The length of her shadow after 4 seconds is 1.6 metres (160 cm).

Board Exam Tip: Convert all measurements to meters or cm before setting up the similarity ratio.
