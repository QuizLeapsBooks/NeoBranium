Shubham Singh — Founder

Creator of NeoBranium • By Aevorin

Shubham Singh is the founder and creator of NeoBranium, an AI-augmented educational ecosystem built under the Aevorin brand to empower students across India with world-class, accessible learning technology.

The Vision Behind NeoBranium

NeoBranium was founded by Shubham Singh out of a simple yet profound realization: every student deserves an inspiring, patient, and deeply knowledgeable learning companion. Traditional learning resources are often fragmented, difficult to navigate, or locked behind costly paywalls.

Shubham designed NeoBranium as a unified, student-first sanctuary. The platform harmonizes rigorous academic curricula—ranging from NCERT/CBSE Class 9 through 12—with real-time multimodal Artificial Intelligence. Whether a learner is untangling complex chemical reactions, mastering trigonometric proofs, or revising during late-night exam prep, NeoBranium turns confusion into clarity.

AI Innovations Engineered by the Founder

Under Shubham Singh's leadership and technical architecture, NeoBranium has pioneered several flagship tools tailored specifically to the needs of students:

NeoBot Live AI

Real-time conversational voice and vision intelligence delivering interactive, bidirectional study mentoring and conceptual coaching.

NeoLens Solver

Computer-vision question solver that extracts handwritten or printed problems and generates step-by-step solutions without confusing jargon.

NeoBoard Canvas

An infinite interactive whiteboard enabling learners to brainstorm, sketch diagrams, and explore mind maps alongside AI guidance.

Exam Engine

Intelligent test and question paper generator aligned with CBSE standards, blueprint schemes, and Bloom's taxonomy.

Core Philosophy & Mission

1. Universal Accessibility: High-quality learning tools should be freely accessible to every ambitious student, regardless of geography or financial background.

2. Conceptual Depth Over Rote Memorization: Tools are crafted to instill deep fundamental understanding, encouraging students to ask "why" and "how" instead of merely memorizing answers.

3. Continuous Evolution: NeoBranium is an ever-evolving ecosystem, continually updated with state-of-the-art AI advancements, verified curriculum resources, and feedback directly from active learners.
