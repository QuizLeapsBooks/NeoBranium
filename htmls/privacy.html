Privacy Policy

At NeoBranium, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe learning environment for all users, including students and minors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email Address: Collected during registration or login.
  • Usage Data: Includes pages visited, features used, and interaction logs.
  • Uploaded Content: Such as PDFs, answers, or study materials you submit (after approval).
  • Cookies: Used to enhance your experience and remember preferences.

2. Why We Collect Data

  • To create and manage your account (login, authentication).
  • To personalize your learning experience and recommend content.
  • To improve our platform, features, and services.
  • To communicate important updates or respond to your queries.
  • To ensure safety and prevent misuse.

3. How We Protect Your Data

  • All sensitive data (like passwords) is encrypted and securely stored.
  • We use Firebase Authentication and secure cloud storage.
  • Access to your data is strictly controlled and monitored.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services to enhance your experience, such as:

  • Google AdSense: For displaying relevant ads.
  • Analytics Tools: To understand usage and improve our platform.
  • AI APIs (e.g., Gemini, OpenAI): For features like doubt solving and answer checking.

These services may collect certain data as per their own privacy policies.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and analyze site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. User Rights

  • You can access, update, or delete your account at any time.
  • You may request removal of your data by contacting us.
  • We will respond to such requests in a reasonable time frame.

7. Children’s Privacy

NeoBranium is designed for students, including minors. We do not knowingly collect more information than necessary. If you are under 18, please use the platform with parental guidance. Parents/guardians may contact us to review or delete their child’s data.

8. Data Selling Policy

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified on our website.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your privacy, please email us at: support@neobranium.com
