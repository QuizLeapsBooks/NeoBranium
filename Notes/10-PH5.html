Quick Revision Formula Sheet – Last Minute Exam Prep!
All important formulas from Physics (Light, Electricity, Magnetic Effects) and Chemistry (Chemical Reactions, Acids-Bases, Metals & Non-metals, Carbon Compounds) in one place for CBSE Class 10 Board Exam 2025-26.
PHYSICS

Physics Formulas High Priority

Light – Reflection (Mirrors)

1/v + 1/u = 1/f
m = -v/u
Mirror Formula: f = focal length, u = object distance (-ve), v = image distance. m = magnification (positive for virtual, negative for real).
For concave mirror: f is negative. For convex mirror: f is positive.

Light – Refraction (Lenses)

1/v - 1/u = 1/f
m = v/u
P = 1/f (in meters)
Lens formula (sign convention: u always -ve for real objects). Power P in Dioptre (D). Convex lens: f positive, P positive. Concave lens: f negative, P negative.

Refractive Index

n = c / v
n = sin i / sin r (Snell's Law)
n₂₁ = n₂ / n₁ = v₁ / v₂
n = absolute refractive index, c = speed of light in vacuum (3×10⁸ m/s), v = speed in medium. Higher n means optically denser medium.

Electricity – Ohm's Law

V = I × R
R = ρ × L / A
V = Potential difference (Volts), I = Current (Amperes), R = Resistance (Ohms Ω). ρ = Resistivity (Ω·m), L = length, A = cross-sectional area.
Resistivity depends on material and temperature, not on dimensions.

Resistance Combinations

Series: Rₛ = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ + ...
Parallel: 1/Rₚ = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃ + ...
In series: current same, voltage divides. In parallel: voltage same, current divides.

Electric Power & Energy

P = V × I = I²R = V²/R
Energy (E) = P × t
1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
P in Watts, t in seconds for Joules. Commercial unit: kilowatt-hour (kWh).

Heating Effect of Current

H = I² × R × t
H = V × I × t = V²t / R
H = Heat produced (Joules). Application: electric heater, fuse wire.

Magnetic Effects

Fleming's Left Hand Rule (Motor)
Fleming's Right Hand Rule (Generator)
EMF = Rate of change of magnetic flux
No direct formula but rules are crucial. Transformer: Vₚ/Vₛ = Nₚ/Nₛ = Iₛ/Iₚ (ideal).
CHEMISTRY

Chemistry Formulas & Important Points Exam Essentials

Types of Reactions

Combination: A + B → AB
Decomposition: AB → A + B
Displacement: A + BC → AC + B
Double Displacement: AB + CD → AD + CB
Redox: Oxidation (loss of e⁻), Reduction (gain of e⁻). OIL RIG.

pH Scale & Concentration

pH = -log₁₀[H⁺]
pOH = -log₁₀[OH⁻]
pH + pOH = 14 (at 25°C)
pH < 7: Acidic, pH = 7: Neutral, pH > 7: Basic. Universal indicator shows pH range.
Acidic: turns blue litmus red. Basic: turns red litmus blue.

Important Salts & Formulae

Baking Soda: NaHCO₃
Washing Soda: Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
Bleaching Powder: CaOCl₂
POP (Plaster of Paris): CaSO₄·½H₂O
Caustic Soda: NaOH
Caustic Potash: KOH
POP on heating with water sets into hard mass (CaSO₄·2H₂O - Gypsum).

Reactivity Series (Metals)

K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > H > Cu > Ag > Au
Mnemonic: "King Naughty Cats Make A Zebra In The Happy Cage Called Asian Aquarium"
Metals above H displace H₂ from acids. More reactive metals displace less reactive ones.

Carbon Compounds

Catenation: Self-linking of carbon atoms
Homologous Series: General formula CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ (Alkanes)
Functional Groups: -OH (Alcohol), -CHO (Aldehyde), -COOH (Carboxylic acid)
Soaps: Sodium salts of fatty acids (micelle formation). Detergents: ammonium/sulphonate salts – work in hard water.

Must-Know Chemical Equations

Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
Rusting: 4Fe + 3O₂ + 2xH₂O → 2Fe₂O₃·xH₂O
Chlor-alkali: 2NaCl + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + Cl₂ + H₂
Baking soda prep: NaCl + H₂O + NH₃ + CO₂ → NaHCO₃ + NH₄Cl
Balance these equations – often in 3-mark questions!

Formula Writing Rules

Criss-cross method for ionic compounds
Example: Al³⁺ + O²⁻ → Al₂O₃
Prefixes: mono, di, tri, tetra (covalent compounds)
Valency of common ions: Na⁺, Ca²⁺, Al³⁺, Cl⁻, O²⁻, N³⁻, SO₄²⁻, NO₃⁻, CO₃²⁻, PO₄³⁻.
Quick Reference: Common Conversions & Constants
• 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J    • Speed of light (c) = 3 × 10⁸ m/s    • 1 Dioptre = 1 m⁻¹
• 1 Ampere = 1 Coulomb/second    • 1 Volt = 1 Joule/Coulomb    • 1 Ohm = 1 Volt/Ampere
• Avogadro's number = 6.022 × 10²³    • Room temperature = 25°C = 298 K
Exam Tips:
• Always write sign conventions in mirror/lens problems.
• In electricity, remember to convert units (mA to A, kW to W).
• For chemistry, write balanced equations with state symbols (s, l, g, aq).
• pH = -log[H⁺] is rarely asked for calculation but concept is important.
• Practice numericals on lens formula, mirror formula, electricity (power, resistance), and heating effect.
